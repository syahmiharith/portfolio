<h2 id="decisions">Three decisions, in the product</h2>

<Decision number="01" title="Keep the source beside the correction" summary="Inspect the image and edit the expense in the same place." screen="receipt-correction">

Corrections save as they are entered. Image zoom, rotation and keyboard panning support inspection; small screens switch between image and form. Long merchant and item names grow to fit, while quantities, prices and searchable currency stay compact.

Original extraction confidence stays separate from human edits. A correction does not become a new confidence score.

</Decision>

<Decision number="02" title="Keep successful work when a batch fails" summary="Each receipt has its own state. Retrying a failure must preserve the others." screen="receipt-batch">

Uploads, ZIP selection and optional samples use the same queue, with up to five images and two extraction slots. Failed-only retries retain successful values and corrections. Stop finishes active requests and leaves the rest queued; refresh restores saved work and makes interrupted requests retryable.

The capture shows two ready samples. Failure recovery is demonstrated by the linked regression release below.

</Decision>

<Decision number="03" title="Export the values people can see" summary="One menu exports the selected, valid expenses as CSV or JSON." screen="receipt-export">

CSV gives each receipt one row with current expense details and line items. Receipt IDs and source filenames connect the output to its image; JSON also retains original extraction and confidence. Validation flags missing dates, currency and totals before export, and spreadsheet text is protected against formula execution.

</Decision>

<h2 id="engineering">Build, maintain, verify</h2>

## What I designed and built

I own the receipt editor, image inspection, batch processing, browser recovery, extraction integration and spreadsheet output. Next.js, React and TypeScript power the application. IndexedDB keeps images and corrections on the device; shared Redis limits protect live extraction. Saved work expires after seven days without an edit and can be deleted from the app.

### Improvements carried through to release

Samples and uploads now share an editor, corrections save automatically, and one batch toolbar replaces separate download controls. Runtime checks also led to mobile wrapping fixes, searchable currency, ZIP selection and removal of repeated notes.

### Checks tied to real tasks

Regression checks correct a total, refresh, inspect the downloaded CSV, and retry a mixed batch without resending the successful receipt. They compare receipt IDs and original confidence across the journey. The linked releases provide the implementation evidence.

### Verification scope

Product screens use synthetic samples captured on 9 November 2026. The editor and linked regression evidence were reviewed on 8 November. No measured extraction accuracy, customer adoption or time-saving result is claimed.
